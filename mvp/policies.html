Shipping Policy

Effective: 1 January 2026

Delivery Areas

We deliver to all 36 states and the FCT (Abuja) in Nigeria. International shipping is also available — please contact us on WhatsApp or at support@pheran.ng for a delivery quote before placing your order.

Delivery Methods & Costs

  • Standard Delivery — ₦1,500 · 3–5 business days
  • Express Delivery — ₦3,500 · 1–2 business days
  • Free Standard Delivery on all orders above ₦100,000
  • International Delivery — fee quoted on request; contact us before ordering

Business days are Monday to Saturday, excluding Nigerian public holidays.

Processing Time

Ready-to-wear orders are processed within 1–2 business days before dispatch. Custom-made orders require 10–14 business days for production (5–7 with rush processing at an additional charge of ₦15,000).

Tracking

You will receive an SMS and email with a tracking link once your order has been dispatched. You can also check your order status in your account dashboard.

Failed Delivery

If delivery is unsuccessful, our courier will make two further attempts before returning the package to our facility. We will contact you to arrange re-delivery. Re-delivery incurs a ₦1,000 fee.

Lost or Damaged Parcels

If your order arrives damaged or goes missing in transit, contact us within 48 hours at support@pheran.ng or via WhatsApp. We will arrange a replacement or full refund at our expense.

Returns & Refunds

Effective: 1 January 2026

Eligibility

We accept returns within 24 hours of delivery for ready-to-wear items that are:

  • Unworn and unwashed
  • In original condition with all tags attached
  • In the original packaging

Non-Returnable Items

  • Custom-made and bespoke pieces (unless there is a manufacturing defect)
  • Items marked "Final Sale" at time of purchase
  • Intimates and swimwear (for hygiene reasons)
  • Gift cards

How to Initiate a Return

  1. Log in to your account and navigate to your order
  2. Click "Request Return" and select the items and reason
  3. You'll receive a return label within 24 hours
  4. Drop the package at any courier location or arrange a pickup

Refund Processing

Once we receive and inspect your return (within 2 business days of receipt), we will process your refund. Funds will appear in your original payment account within 5–10 business days depending on your bank.

Exchanges

We offer size and colour exchanges for in-stock items. Initiate an exchange the same way as a return, and select "Exchange" instead of "Refund." No additional shipping fee is charged for your first exchange per order.

Defective or Incorrect Items

If you receive a damaged, defective, or incorrect item, please contact us within 48 hours of delivery. We will cover all return shipping costs and send a replacement or issue a full refund — your choice.

Privacy Policy

Effective: 1 January 2026

What We Collect

When you use PHERAN, we may collect:

  • Account information — name, email, phone number, password (hashed)
  • Order information — shipping address, order history, payment method type (not card details)
  • Browsing data — pages visited, products viewed, time on site (via anonymised analytics)
  • Communications — messages you send us via email, WhatsApp, or contact forms

How We Use Your Data

  • To process and fulfil your orders
  • To personalise your shopping experience and recommend products
  • To send transactional emails (order confirmations, shipping updates)
  • To send marketing communications (only with your explicit consent)
  • To improve our website and services

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with:

  • Delivery partners — your name and address for shipping
  • Payment processors — Paystack, Flutterwave (they process, not store, your card details)
  • Analytics providers — anonymised, aggregated data only

Data Retention

We retain your order data for 7 years for legal and accounting purposes. You may request deletion of personal data not required for legal compliance at any time.

Your Rights

  • Right to access your personal data
  • Right to correct inaccurate data
  • Right to delete your account and associated data
  • Right to opt out of marketing communications at any time

To exercise any of these rights, email privacy@pheran.ng.

Cookies

We use essential cookies to keep you logged in and maintain your cart. We also use analytics cookies to understand how our site is used. You can manage cookie preferences in your browser settings.

Security

All data is transmitted over HTTPS. Card payments are handled entirely by Paystack and Flutterwave — your card details never touch our servers. We conduct regular security audits and maintain industry-standard access controls.
